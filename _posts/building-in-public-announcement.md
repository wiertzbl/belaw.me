With this post I will start building in public.

After 3 years in venture capital, and spending the last year building {Tech: Berlin}, I decided that I want to take 2025 to focus on building my own venture. I recognized that (at least for me) its not possible to do this on the side, and therefore I will consider this my full-time challenge.

I start with a few ideas (0 of them validated) and no co-founder (but a few discussions with people here and there).

I will use this blog to document my journey and share my learnings & progress.

The first 3-4 months will be dedicated only to problem search & validation. Until end of April I want to have 1 validated problem that I want to go full into. Finding a co-founder is not a hard requirement for me, as I am not sure if I want to build a vc-backed or a bootstrapped company. 

Hope you have fun following my journey. I'll try to make bi-weekly updates.

Cheers,
